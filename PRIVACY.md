# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 11, 2026**

AI Health Connector ("the app") is a personal health data integration project that allows users to connect supported health data sources, including Oura, and use their authorized data for personal health analysis and related features.

## Information We Access

The app only accesses data after the user explicitly authorizes access through the relevant provider's authorization flow, such as Oura OAuth.

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, the app may access health and wellness data such as:

- Sleep and sleep-related metrics
- Heart rate and resting heart rate
- Heart rate variability (HRV)
- Activity and workout data
- Blood oxygen (SpO2) data
- Readiness, recovery, or similar wellness metrics when available
- Other health-related data made available by the connected provider and explicitly authorized by the user

The app may also store provider connection information, such as access tokens and refresh tokens, as necessary to maintain the authorized connection.

## How We Use Information

Authorized health data is used only to provide functionality requested by the user, including:

- Retrieving and organizing personal health data
- Showing health trends and summaries
- Comparing metrics over time
- Calculating personal baselines and statistical changes
- Supporting AI-assisted analysis of the user's own health and wellness data

We do not use personal health data for advertising.

## Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell personal data or health data.

We do not share personal health data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data may be processed by infrastructure or service providers only when necessary to operate the app and provide requested functionality. Any such processing is limited to the purposes required to provide the service.

## Data Storage and Security

The app is designed to use reasonable technical safeguards to protect stored information. Sensitive credentials, such as provider access tokens and refresh tokens, should be stored securely and encrypted where applicable.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## User Control and Revocation

Users may revoke the app's access to a connected service, such as Oura, through that provider's account or authorization settings.

Revoking access prevents the app from obtaining new data from that provider.

Users may also request deletion of health data and connection information stored by the app.

## Data Retention

Health data and connection information are retained only for as long as needed to provide the requested functionality, support the user's account or connection, or satisfy applicable legal requirements.

For development and testing use, stored data may be deleted when it is no longer needed.

## Third-Party Services

The app may connect to third-party health platforms such as Oura. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. This Privacy Policy applies only to data handled by AI Health Connector after the user authorizes access.

## Medical Disclaimer

AI Health Connector is intended for informational and personal wellness purposes. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this document.
